About Council 626

The Knights of Columbus Council 626 is a Catholic fraternal service organization dedicated to serving our community in Norwalk, Ohio. We are committed to the principles of charity, unity, fraternity, and patriotism.

Our council provides opportunities for Catholic men to serve their community, support their parish, and grow in their faith alongside brother Knights.
